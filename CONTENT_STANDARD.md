# Growth Terminal Social Content Standard

Effective 2026-10-01.

## Non-negotiable editorial rule

Growth Terminal social posts must be informative and directly related to the business:
- interactive websites
- product launches
- 3D scroll experiences
- digital product storytelling
- conversion and buyer understanding as they relate to those services

Every post must teach the audience something useful: a framework, breakdown, mistake, decision rule, before/after explanation, process, or practical lesson.

## Allowed references

Only these two content references are allowed:
- DIGI MONK
- Alex Hormozi

Use them as strategic references for clarity, education, strong hooks, concrete business lessons, and actionable takeaways. Do not copy their wording, layouts, branding, or identity.

## Visual rule

Generate new visuals that explain the lesson. Do not use Growth Terminal project assets as social filler. Visuals must help the audience understand interactive websites, product launches, or 3D scroll experiences.

## Banned

- generic AI moodboards
- random luxury objects or unrelated imagery
- motivational filler
- generic agency inspiration
- project-asset recycling
- unrelated trend posts
- invented case-study results
- fake metrics or unsupported claims
- references to any creator, agency, or content style other than DIGI MONK and Alex Hormozi

A post that does not teach something directly useful to a prospective Growth Terminal buyer does not ship.
